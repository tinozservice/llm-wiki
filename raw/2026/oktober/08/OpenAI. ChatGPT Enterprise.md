## Enterprise-grade security built in

### We never train on your data and your data is secure

Your business data, including conversations, files, and outputs from ChatGPT, is not used to train our models by default. ChatGPT Enterprise supports encryption, retention, and compliance controls to help protect sensitive work.

### You control access to your company data and tools

Admins can manage who can use agents, connected tools, and actions across the workspace.

### We comply with industry standards and regulations

ChatGPT Enterprise supports compliance requirements with centralized visibility, auditability, and governance controls, including Compliance API visibility for conversations and agent activity.
