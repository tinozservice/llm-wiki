# Groq — Security & Vulnerability Disclosure

- **Sumber**: groq.com/security
- **Penulis**: groq.com
- **URL**: <https://groq.com/security>
- **Tanggal publikasi**: tidak dicantumkan; klip dibuat 2026-10-08

## TL;DR

Halaman keamanan Groq: **Groq Trust Center** (trust.groq.com) untuk program keamanan informasi & compliance; **program private vulnerability disclosure via HackerOne** (invitation; kirim username HackerOne ke security@groq.com); reward "at Groq's discretion". Eksplisit: **jailbreak/prompt bypass & halusinasi model = out of scope**.

## Key points

- Panduan peneliti: ≥18 tahun; bukan penduduk negara embargo/sanctions; good faith (tanpa gangguan layanan/data); hanya temuan orisinal & belum dilaporkan; cukup informasi repro; kooperasi sebelum disclosure publik.
- Isu pihak ketiga bisa dibagikan ke vendor terkait untuk remediasi terkoordinasi.
- **Out of scope**: prompt-based safety bypass, jailbreak, output berbahaya; **model hallucinations/simulasi** (klaim palsu mengakses sistem/menjalankan kode) — juga di luar lingkup.
- Submisi tunduk pada [Terms of Use](groq-terms-of-use.md).

## Notable quotes

> "Issues related solely to model prompts, model-generated responses, jailbreaks, prompt-based safety bypass techniques… are out of scope."
