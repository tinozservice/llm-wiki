## Organization Limits

Base rate limits for your organization. These can be customized per project.  
On [Developer plan](https://console.groq.com/settings/billing), you get higher limits and can request additional limit increases.

### Allow or Block Models

Choose which models to allow or block for this organization. [Learn more](https://console.groq.com/docs/model-permissions) about model permissions.
