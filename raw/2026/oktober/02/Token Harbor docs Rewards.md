## Rewards

Token Harbor keeps incentives simple. There is **no per-call cashback** and **no volume discount** — value comes from passthrough per-token pricing, the platform caches, and the rewards below. All rewards and promotional credits count toward a **$500 lifetime cap** per account.

## First top-up match

Your **first** wallet top-up is matched 100%, up to **$100**:

| Top-up | Bonus | You get |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $10 | +$10 | $20 |
| $50 | +$50 | $100 |
| $100 | +$100 | $200 |

- Available for **14 days** after you register.
- The bonus is **promotional credit** (spendable on any model, not withdrawable as cash).
- It is credited **as soon as your top-up completes** and is spendable straight away — there is no lock, no unlock schedule, no holding window and no claim button.

## Spend milestones

As your **lifetime** spend grows, bonus credit unlocks automatically — each milestone once:

| Lifetime spend | Bonus credit |
| --- | --- |
| $10 | $2 |
| $50 | $10 |
| $200 | $50 |

Milestones never reset and are credited the moment you cross the threshold.

## Free model access

Selected models are available through explicit `:free` model IDs — the current set is listed under **Free** on the [Models](https://tokenharbor.ai/models) page and changes as models are added or retired. Your first free request starts a personal rolling **7×24-hour** period; it is not tied to a calendar week or midnight UTC.

The allowance is measured by the list-price value of the work rather than a fixed number of requests. The dashboard shows a 0–100% progress bar. Free routes never charge your balance; the base model IDs remain paid routes.

Some models are opened up through a **campaign** instead. A campaign is a limited-time free route with an end date, and it is metered in one of two ways: either it carries its own separate allowance, or it draws on the free-tier allowance above. When one is running for your account it appears on the [dashboard](https://tokenharbor.ai/dashboard) with a progress bar that says which of the two it is, and the time that allowance next refreshes. A campaign that shares the free-tier allowance shows the same percentage as the free-tier bar, because it is the same allowance. Which models are in a campaign, how they are metered, and for how long, changes over time.

Free-route prompts and responses may be retained by Token Harbor only after you enable free models. See [Privacy](https://tokenharbor.ai/privacy) for details. Models and limits may change over time.

## Starting balance & invites

New accounts start at **$0** — there is no sign-up or welcome credit (see [Credits & top-ups](https://tokenharbor.ai/docs/billing/credits)). Every account still gets a permanent invite code for bringing friends in, but there is no cash referral reward at the moment (see [Referrals](https://tokenharbor.ai/docs/billing/referrals)).
