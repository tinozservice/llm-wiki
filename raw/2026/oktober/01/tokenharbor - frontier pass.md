## Usage value, not credit

Measured at our published per-token prices — what the same traffic would have cost without a pass. Not a wallet balance. A month here is 4 windows of 7 days: a quarter of the figure in each, and unused room does not carry forward.

## Your free allowance carries over

Subscribing does not replace the free allowance you already have. It is added on top of the pass, in one pool with one progress bar on your dashboard.

## Nothing stops at the limit

Use the month’s value up and calls keep working, billed from your balance at your pass’s discounted per-token rate. A pass is included usage, not a cap on your account.
