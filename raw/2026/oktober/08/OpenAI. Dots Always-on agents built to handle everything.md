## Get going with dots

Think of your dot as an extension of you. It learns how you work, spots what needs attention, and uses its own computer to move your projects forward.

### Your dot works the way you do

Your dot starts with context from your ChatGPT memory. It uses Codex and your connected tools to take on tasks. You choose which apps it can access, what it should focus on, and how you like things done.

### Give it a responsibility to own

Hand over a big project or something smaller to take off your plate. Your dot proactively figures out the next steps and keeps making progress between conversations. It brings you work to review and decisions that need your judgment.

### You’re in charge

See what your dot is working on, review the details, and give feedback. Change direction or pause anytime.

### Bring your dot to work

Your dot can help wherever you work. You manage its permissions and decide when it can act on its own or needs your approval.

## On your side, under your control

Dots run on GPT-6 Astra, our most aligned model, with safety and privacy protections on by default.

### Built-in protections

Dots build on ChatGPT’s protections with safeguards that help defend against malicious instructions and watch for potentially harmful behavior.

### Your boundaries

Dots work on their own cloud computers. Choose which apps to connect, manage their permissions, and decide whether to connect your computer. Set Custom Rules to allow specific actions, require approval, or block them.

### Checks that matter

Actions that could affect your accounts or share information go through Auto-review for checks against your instructions, Custom Rules, and safety requirements. Some need your approval. Steps like changing your password stay with you.
