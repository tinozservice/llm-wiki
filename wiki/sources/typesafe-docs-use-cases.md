## TL;DR

TypeSafe memposisikan Jev untuk lima kategori besar: **AI automation software** (berjalan sejuta kali tanpa co-pilot), **real-time applications** (keputusan di **150 ms**), **AI map-reduce atas data besar** (klaim **100× lebih murah**), **universal verification** (verifikasi prompt/output AI lain), dan **harness engineering** (routing model, guardrails, klasifikasi trace). Peta industri mencakup ~15 domain dari customer support hingga knowledge graph.

## Key points

- Kategori otomasi: search/retrieval (alternatif embedding di RAG, reranking), scientific discovery (screening paper, anotasi transkrip), **model routing** (memilih LLM penerima prompt), **LLM guardrails** (jailbreak, prompt injection, data sensitif, error tool-call), semantic code linting, feature extraction untuk model prediktif klasik.
- Industri: recruiting, lead generation, customer support, insurance claims, financial crime (KYC/AML), legal & compliance, e-commerce, moderation & trust and safety, gaming, risk assessment, demand forecasting, graphs/knowledge graphs.
- Tabel taksonomi tugas: **classification, detection, scoring, routing, search, retrieval, ranking, verification, ML feature extraction, structured data extraction** — masing-masing dengan contoh.
- Contoh konkret dari kategori real-time & verification dipakai lagi di cookbook (bukan bagian klip ini).
