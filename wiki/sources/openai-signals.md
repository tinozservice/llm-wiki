## TL;DR

**OpenAI Signals** — hub data/riset/cerita dari **OpenAI Economic Research Team**: pola adopsi ChatGPT individual global, distribusi geografis, penggunaan kerja vs non-kerja; **Enterprise Signals** melacak adopsi AI lintas industri & fungsi (frontier gap, agentic work). Berita: "How workers are unlocking new ways of working", "Research acceleration: view inside OpenAI", "What students gain from ChatGPT and critical-thinking training"; terkait **OpenAI Economic Research Exchange**.
