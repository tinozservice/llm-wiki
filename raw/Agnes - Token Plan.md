## FAQs

Common questions about Agnes subscription plans, model quotas, and features

[More questions →](https://www.agnes-ai.com/doc/tokenplan)

- What is Agnes-3.0-Flash? Do the three plans use different models?
	Agnes-3.0-Flash is the current underlying inference model. All three plans (Starter, Plus, and Pro) use the same model and offer identical model capabilities. The main differences are the 5-hour request quota, RPM limits, and pricing.
- What are the differences between Starter, Plus, and Pro?
	The main difference is the number of model requests available within a 5-hour window: 1,500 for Starter, 7,500 for Plus, and 30,000 for Pro. All three plans use the same underlying model and offer the same response speed and multimodal capabilities.
- What does “model requests / 5 hours” mean? How is the quota calculated?
	It refers to the maximum number of model requests you can make within any rolling 5-hour window. As time moves forward, requests made more than 5 hours ago no longer count toward the limit, so your quota is gradually restored. It is not divided into fixed 5-hour time blocks.
- What happens when I reach the quota limit?
	Once you reach the limit, you will not be able to make additional model requests until earlier requests roll out of the 5-hour window. If you need a higher quota, you can upgrade to a higher-tier plan.
- What is the TPS? Is it faster during off-peak hours?
	The typical generation speed is around 100 TPS (tokens per second) and may reach approximately 150 TPS during off-peak hours. Performance may fluctuate slightly during peak periods.
- Which development tools and features are supported?
	Compatible with mainstream coding tools.
- Should I choose a monthly or annual subscription?
	The annual subscription effectively gives you two months free, which is approximately 17% cheaper than paying monthly. It is ideal for long-term users, while the monthly subscription offers greater flexibility and can be canceled at any time. If you plan to use the service long term, the annual plan offers better value.
