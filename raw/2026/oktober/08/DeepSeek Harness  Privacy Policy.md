## Introduction

This privacy policy ("Privacy Policy") applies to the Personal Data (or other similar terms as defined by applicable data protection law) relating to you ("Personal Data") that DeepSeek processes in connection with DeepSeek Harness software, and related services (the "Services"), that link to or reference this Privacy Policy. The Services enable you to call models and tools to perform tasks.

Data Controller: The Services are provided and controlled by **Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd.** ("we" or "us"). If you have any questions about how we use your Personal Data, please contact [harness-privacy@deepseek.com](mailto:harness-privacy@deepseek.com) or click the "Contact us" button on the product interface.

Please be informed that DeepSeek Harness offers two categories of services: official model services and custom model services. When you use the custom model services, you do not need to register or log in. You obtain and configure the API of other large models yourself; in that case, DeepSeek Harness only provides the interaction medium for model calls, including the locally run interactive interface, supporting tools, and communication technologies. Your Input is sent directly to the relevant large model for processing, and the Output is returned directly by that large model; DeepSeek Harness does not store or process your Input and Output on the server side. The processing of your Personal Data in that scenario is governed by the relevant model provider's own policies, and the model provider, as the controller of that Personal Data processing activity, should disclose its Personal Data protection rules to you.

## What Personal Data We Collect

We collect your Personal Data in three ways: Personal Data You Provide, Automatically Collected Personal Data, and Personal Data From Other Sources. More detail is provided below.

### Personal Data You Provide

- **Account Personal Data.** We collect Personal Data that you provide when you set up an DeepSeek account, such as email address and/or telephone number. Please note that the DeepSeek application, the Open Platform, and DeepSeek Harness all use a unified DeepSeek account; **a DeepSeek account you register in one service can be used directly to log in to other services.**
- **Session Log** **Data.** When you use DeepSeek Harness to call models and tools to perform work, we collect the session logs generated during your interaction with DeepSeek Harness, including: **User inputs, uploaded and authorized content (including but not limited to text, images, files, and voice), model outputs and tool invocation information, plugin configuration information, and conversation data**.
- **Personal Data** **When You Contact Us.** When you contact us, we collect the Personal Data you send to us, such as proof of identity or age, contact details, feedback or inquiries about your use of the Services or Personal Data about possible violations of our [Terms of Service](https://www.deepseek.com/en/harness/terms-of-use/) (our “ **Terms** ”) or other policies.

### Automatically Collected Personal Data

We automatically collect certain Personal Data from you when you use the Services, including network logs and device-related information.

- **Device and Network Personal Data.** We collect certain device and network connection Personal Data when you access the Services. This Personal Data includes your device model, operating system, IP address, device identifiers and system language. We also collect service-related, diagnostic, and performance Personal Data, including crash reports and performance logs. We automatically assign you a device ID and user ID. Where you log-in from multiple devices, we use Personal Data such as your device ID and user ID to identify your activity across devices to give you a seamless log-in experience and for security purposes.
- **Log Personal Data**. We collect Personal Data regarding your use of the Services, such as the features you use and the actions you take.
- **Location Personal Data**. We automatically collect Personal Data about your approximate location based on IP address for security reasons, for example to protect your account by detecting unusual login activity.
- **Cookies & Similar Technologies**. We may use cookies and similar tracking technologies to operate and provide the Service. For example, we use cookies for security purposes and to better understand how the Service is used. We will allow you to manage our use of non-essential cookies where required by law.
- **Payment Personal Data**. When you use paid services in our open platform, we collect your payment order and transaction Personal Data to provide Services such as order placement, payment, customer service, and after-sales support.

### Personal Data from Other Sources

**Log-in, Sign-up, or Linked Services.** You may use a third-party account to log in to and use the DeepSeek account; with your consent, we will obtain the public information registered on the third-party platform (avatar, nickname, and other information you authorize) to bind it to the DeepSeek account, so that you can directly log in to and use the products and related services.

The Services are not designed or intended to process sensitive Personal Data. **We do not ask for, and you should not provide, sensitive** **Personal Data** **to the Services, whether about yourself or other individuals.** If the information you actively provide contains other people's Personal Data, you must obtain their lawful authorization in advance to avoid improper disclosure of their Personal Data.

This could include providing law enforcement agencies or emergency services with Personal Data in urgent situations to protect health or life, in accordance with the due process prescribed by law.
- **PLEASE NOTE: We do not engage in “profiling” or otherwise engage in automated processing of Personal Data to make decisions that could have legal or similarly significant impact on you or others.**

You can control and access some of your Personal Data directly through settings. For example, you can manage your chat history. Should you choose to do so, you may also copy your chat history via your settings.

If you choose to delete your account, you will not be able to reactivate your account or retrieve any of the content or Personal Data in connection with your account.

**For this reason, you should not rely on the factual accuracy of Output from our models. If you notice that Output contains factually inaccurate Personal Data about you and you would like to request a correction or removal of the Personal Data, you can submit these requests through** **[harness-privacy@deepseek.com](mailto:harness-privacy@deepseek.com)****.and we will consider your request based on applicable law and the technical capabilities of our models.**
