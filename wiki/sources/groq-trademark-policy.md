## TL;DR

Kebijakan merek **Groq LLC**: mark **GROQ, GroqCloud, GroqChat, GroqConsole** + formatif GROQ- lain milik Groq; penggunaan pihak ketiga hanya via **nominative fair use** (referensi faktual, tanpa logo, tanpa kesan endorsement) atau izin tertulis; wajib atribusi "Groq is a trademark of Groq LLC and/or its affiliates."

## Key points

- Dilarang: kesan partnership/certification tanpa persetujuan; penggunaan defamatory/menyesatkan; logo di iklan/packaging/UI tanpa lisensi; mark/domain yang membingungkan.
- Untuk pernyataan faktual: tambahkan disclaimer "…not affiliated with or endorsed by Groq LLC"; klaim performa harus terverifikasi.
- Permintaan izin ke **trademark-legal@groq.com** (nama, perusahaan, jabatan, mark, mockup, konteks, timing, atribusi); badge khusus lewat program terpisah.
- Pelaporan penyalahgunaan juga ke trademark-legal@groq.com; kontak pos: Groq LLC, P.O. Box 1778, Mountain View, CA.

## Notable quotes

> "Except for limited 'nominative fair use', you must not use Groq Marks without prior written permission from Groq."
