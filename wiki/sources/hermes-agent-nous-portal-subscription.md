## TL;DR

Rincian tier Nous Portal (semua tier berbayar: kredit bulanan untuk Hermes Agent, **200+ model**, hosted tool usage, rate limit tinggi):

| Tier | Harga/bln | Kredit bulanan | Rollover cap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | **$0** (model gratis saja) | — |
| Plus | $20 | **$22** (+10%) | $10 |
| Super | $100 | **$110** (+10%) | $50 |
| Ultra | $200 | **$220** (+10%) | $100 |

## Key points

- Model ekonomi: bonus 10% pada kredit; rollover cap membatasi akumulasi tak terpakai (Plus $10 / Super $50 / Ultra $100).
- Free = hanya model gratis + rate limit standar.
- Konsisten dengan halaman landing Hermes (Free/Plus/Super/Ultra) — angka kredit & rollover ini lebih rinci.
