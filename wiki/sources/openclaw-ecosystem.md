# OpenClaw — Ecosystem (65 proyek open source)

- **Sumber**: openclaw.ai/ecosystem

## TL;DR

Peta **65 proyek open source** di sekitar OpenClaw — bot hosted, crawler local-first, SDK, registry skill, tool native — semuanya berbagi satu runtime. Kategori utama: **Agents & Services**, **Local-First Crawlers**, **Agent Infrastructure & SDKs**, **TypeScript Libraries**, **Native Tools**, dan 21 proyek "semuanya".

## Key points

- **Agents & services**: ClawSweeper (triage issue/PR), Crabbox (dev box sekali pakai), Octopool (relay baca GitHub), Crabfleet (mission control fleet agent), **ClawHub** (registry skill/plugin), ClickClack (chat app), **Lobster** (workflow shell typed local-first).
- **Local-first crawlers** (arsip ke SQLite di laptop): discrawl, gitcrawl, slacrawl, wacrawl, imsgcrawl, photoscrawl, notcrawl, telecrawl, graincrawl + infrastruktur crawlkit & crawlbar.
- **Infrastruktur & SDK**: acpx (ACP client), mcporter (MCP via TS), Tachikoma (Swift SDK), clawpatch (review/patch PR), **clawbench** (benchmark harness+model bersama), **ClawScan** (security scan skill), crabline (mock channel), agent-skills, plugin-inspector, cookbook.
- **Library TS**: rastermill (image), fs-safe (filesystem root-bounded), proxyline, libopus-wasm, ffmpeg-wasm, libterminal, clawpdf.
- **Native tools**: Peekaboo (screenshot/UI macOS), AXorcist (Accessibility), imsg (Apple Messages), wacli (WhatsApp CLI), remindctl, gogcli (Google Workspace), spogo (Spotify), songsee (audio FFT), goplaces.
- **Lainnya**: nix-openclaw, clawdinators, openclaw-ansible (hardened install: Tailscale, UFW, Docker isolation), esp-openclaw-node (node berbasis ESP), openclaw-windows-node (Windows Hub), clawdex, crabpot, kitchen-sink, rfcs, releases, homebrew-tap, dll.
