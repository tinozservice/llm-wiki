When you interact with our Services, we collect your personal information from you when you visit our websites, request information from us, create an account, submit prompts, inputs, or other content, interact with our AI models or APIs, make purchases, or otherwise interact with us through the Services.

The information we collect through the Services may include:

- Personal identifiers and account information, such as name, username, email address, telephone number, billing address, account credentials, payment-related information, organization or company affiliation, and other information provided when creating or managing an account or communicating with us;
- Commercial and transaction information, such as records of subscriptions, purchases, payment history, account activity, pricing tier selections, and related transaction details;
- AI workflow data, such as prompts, instructions, messages, uploads, inputs, outputs, responses, conversation history, tool interactions, inference requests, API calls, telemetry, usage logs, and related interaction data generated through use of the Services;
- AI usage data, such as records regarding how models, agents, tools, or workflows interact with prompts, systems, APIs, environments, or tools, including intermediate actions, tool usage patterns, execution metadata, and related operational or diagnostic information;
- Internet or other electronic activity information, such as IP address, browser type, device identifiers, operating system, device characteristics, referral URLs, interaction with our Services, approximate location derived from IP address, pages viewed, links clicked, session activity, and information regarding interaction with advertisements or analytics tools;

- Audio, visual, or similar information, such as profile images, recordings of support interactions, or other content provided.

We may also collect information from third-party services, integrations, analytics providers, payment processors, authentication providers, and infrastructure providers in connection with use of the Services.

If you create or access your account using a sign-in service provided by a third-party (a “Third-Party Sign-In Service”), we receive the information that service shares with us, such as your name, email address, profile picture and an identifier for your account with that service. We use this information to create, link, authenticate and secure your account and to prevent fraud, and we treat the email address as your account contact. They are used to help users navigate websites efficiently as well as to provide information to the owner of the websites. You may set your browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent; some browsers have other cookie management tools.

When you visit our websites, we may place cookies or other online tracking technologies (e.g., pixels, web beacons) on your browser. Among other things, these help us recognize you when you return to our websites, provide information for analytics, and help us personalize advertisements for you.

## Your Privacy Choices and Opt-Out Rights

We provide users with the ability to limit certain uses of their personal information through the privacy controls made available within the Services. In particular, users may elect to enable “Privacy Mode,” which allows users to opt out of the storage and use of certain inference payloads for training, product improvement, and support purposes.

To enable Privacy Mode:

- Log in to your account at [portal.nousresearch.com](https://portal.nousresearch.com/);
- Navigate to “Account Settings;”
- Locate the “Privacy Settings” section;
- Review the available privacy options; and
- Select “Privacy Mode”

Once Privacy Mode has been selected and the setting has been applied to your account, your preference will be reflected within the Privacy Settings interface. Privacy Mode limits our storage and use of inference payloads, such as prompts, inputs, uploads, messages, outputs, and responses. When Privacy Mode is enabled, we will not store your inference payloads and will not use such inference payloads for training, product improvement, or support purposes, except to the extent necessary to maintain security, integrity, and operation of the Services, comply with applicable law, or detect, prevent, or investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents. Privacy Mode does not prevent us from collecting, storing, or using operational metadata where needed to provide, route, secure, operate, debug, bill for, or otherwise maintain the Services.

Privacy Mode governs Nous’s own storage and use of inference payloads. It does not change how third-party services process information you send to them under that service’s own terms, including through a Third-Party Sign-In Service or a subscription or a connected account provided by such a service, and it does not prevent us from keeping records of actions taken on your connected accounts at your direction where needed to provide, secure and support those features.

Please note that Privacy Mode may apply only on a prospective basis and may not affect information that was collected, processed, incorporated into models, or otherwise used prior to the activation of Privacy Mode. In addition, certain privacy settings or controls may apply only to particular Services or features. Enabling Privacy Mode may impact the functionality, personalization, or availability of certain features of the Services.
