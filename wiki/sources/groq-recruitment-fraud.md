# Groq — Recruitment Fraud Awareness

- **Sumber**: groq.com/recruitment-fraud-awareness

## TL;DR

Peringatan penipuan rekrutmen: scammer menawarkan lowongan fiktif (email tak diminta, LinkedIn, situs palsu, SMS) lalu meminta data pribadi/pembayaran. **Groq tidak pernah**: memakai email non-`@groq.com` (mis. `@groqcareers.com` = scam), meminta data passport/bank di tahap awal, meminta uang (visa/pajak/travel), memakai email gratisan, menekan urgensi, atau mengirim dokumen berantakan.

## Key points

- Semua komunikasi resmi tentang lamaran/interview/offer datang **hanya dari alamat @groq.com**.

## Notable quotes

> "Groq will NEVER… use an email that isn't from the domain @groq.com."
