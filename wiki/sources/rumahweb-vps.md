## TL;DR

VPS KVM Rumahweb dengan lokasi Indonesia, mulai **Rp50.000/bln** (XS: 1 vCPU, 512 MB, 10 GB SSD). Tiga seri: **XS–X4L** (unlimited traffic), **W1–W6** (traffic terbatas, bandwidth besar), **CPU1–CPU6** (CPU/RAM besar). Dua **availability zone**: Zone A TechnoVillage (Tier 3), Zone B DCI (Tier 4). ISO 27001, DDoS protection, aktivasi instan.

## Key points

- Seri X: XS 50k (1/512MB/10GB), S 60k (1/1GB/20), M 120k (1/2GB/40, cPanel Solo gratis), L 245k (2/4GB/80), XL 500k (4/8GB/160), X2L 937.5k (8/16GB/300), X3L 1.875M (8/32GB/600), X4L 3.75M (16/64GB/1.2TB).
- Seri W (traffic 3–10 TB): W1 525k (2/2GB/40), W2 650k (2/4GB/75), W3 1.3M (2/8GB/150), W4 2.6M (4/16GB/300), W5 5.2M (8/32GB/600), W6 10.4M (16/64GB/1.2TB).
- Seri CPU: CPU1 693k (2/4GB/80), CPU2 1.386M (4/8GB/160), CPU3 2.772M (8/16GB/320), CPU4 5.544M (16/32GB/640), CPU5 11.088M (32/64GB/1.28TB), CPU6 16.632M (64/128GB/2.56TB).
- cPanel/Plesk add-on; cPanel Solo gratis untuk paket M ke atas (promo s.d. 16 Nov 2026).
- Infrastruktur: KVM full virtualization, SSD, backend network 1 Gbps, dynamic firewall, DDoS L3/L4, multiple backbone, Tier 3 (99,982%) & Tier 4 (99,995%).
- Manajemen: unmanaged default; Semi Managed gratis dengan cPanel; Managed Service Rp250.000/bln.
- Migrasi: gratis hingga 5 akun cPanel/bulan dari provider lain; Rp50.000/akun di luar kuota. Downgrade tidak tersedia; suspend di jatuh tempo, terminate setelah 7 hari; tanpa refund VPS aktif; port 25 ditutup (whitelist via teknis@).
