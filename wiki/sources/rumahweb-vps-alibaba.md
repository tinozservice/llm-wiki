# Rumahweb — VPS Alibaba Cloud

- **Sumber**: Rumahweb — halaman VPS Alibaba Cloud
- **Penulis**: Rumahweb Indonesia

## TL;DR

VPS dari **Alibaba Cloud** yang dipasarkan Rumahweb: mulai Rp81.400/bln (Linux) dan Rp203.500/bln (Windows); pilihan lokasi **Indonesia, Singapura, US, Jerman**; gratis domain `.CLOUD` tiap pembelian. Bandwidth 200 Mbps (peak), disk SSD. Aktivasi 1–1,5 jam; SLA 99,975%.

## Key points

- **VPS Linux** (2 vCPU, 200 Mbps): 0,5 GB/20 GB Rp81.400; 1 GB/30 GB Rp101.800; 2 GB/40 GB Rp162.800; 4 GB/50 GB Rp325.600; 4 vCPU 8 GB/70 GB Rp651.200; 4 vCPU 16 GB/80 GB Rp976.800. (Varian disk besar: 2 GB/60 GB Rp191.300 dst.)
- **VPS Windows**: 2 GB/40 GB Rp203.500; 4 GB/50 GB Rp407.000; 8 GB/70 GB Rp814.000; 16 GB/80 GB Rp1.221.000. (Varian disk besar tersedia.)
- OS Linux: CentOS 7, Debian 8/9, Ubuntu 16/18, OpenSUSE 42 + pilihan control panel; Windows Server 2012 R2/2016/2019.
- Batasan: port 25 diblokir (relayer bisa port 465); outbound maks 30 Mbps, inbound 100 Mbps; maks 3 snapshot/instance; custom image tidak diizinkan.
- Upgrade bisa, downgrade tidak; tanpa backup otomatis; tanpa bantuan migrasi; tanpa refund; root password diset sendiri di control panel.
